The Biomechanics of 1RM Estimation

A One Repetition Maximum (1RM) is defined in exercise physiology as the maximal amount of weight an athlete can lift for one complete repetition of an exercise with proper technique through a full anatomical range of motion.

Boyd Epley Equation (1985)

Developed by collegiate strength pioneer Boyd Epley at the University of Nebraska. It utilizes a linear 3.33% load reduction per repetition performed:

1RM = Weight × (1 + Reps / 30)

Matt Brzycki Equation (1993)

Published in the Journal of Physical Education, Recreation & Dance. Employs an inverse linear relationship:

1RM = Weight × (36 / (37 - Reps))
