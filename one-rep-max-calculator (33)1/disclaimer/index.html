Medical & Safety Disclaimer

Notice to all athletes, lifters, and coaches

1. Not Medical or Physical Therapy Advice

The content, calculations, formulas, and recommendations presented on this website are provided solely for general fitness and educational purposes. Nothing on this website constitutes medical advice, clinical diagnosis, or individualized sports prescription.

2. Consult a Physician Before Heavy Lifting

Always consult a qualified physician or healthcare provider before beginning any heavy resistance training program or performing maximal lifting attempts, especially if you have pre-existing cardiovascular, musculoskeletal, spinal, or joint conditions.

3. Exercise Safety & Spotters

Maximal barbell lifts (Squats, Bench Presses, Deadlifts, Overhead Presses) carry severe risks including muscle tears, joint trauma, and barbell drop hazards. Always utilize certified safety catches, power racks, collars, and trained spotters when training with heavy working loads.
