Major Lifts & Biomechanics

Biomechanical cues, prime movers, formula recommendations, and 1RM optimization protocols for the most foundational barbell compound movements.

Upper Body Push

Barbell Bench Press

The premier upper body pressing benchmark for horizontal force production.

Primary Muscles Recruited
Pectoralis Major Anterior Deltoid Triceps Brachii
Technical Execution Keys
  • Retract and depress scapulae firmly into the bench pad
  • Maintain 45–70 degree humeral flare angle
  • Drive heels into floor to establish steady leg drive

Lower Body Quad

Barbell Back Squat

The cornerstone of axial loading and lower-body kinetic chain power.

Primary Muscles Recruited
Quadriceps Gluteus Maximus Spinal Erectors
Technical Execution Keys
  • Brace with 360-degree intra-abdominal pressure
  • Descend until hip crease dips below top of patella
  • Drive upward maintaining consistent torso angle

Lower Body Posterior

Conventional Deadlift

The ultimate pure test of full-body posterior chain pull capacity.

Primary Muscles Recruited
Hamstrings Glutes Latissimus Dorsi
Technical Execution Keys
  • Set bar over midfoot, 1 inch from shins
  • Pull slack out of the barbell before breaking floor
  • Push the floor away rather than pulling backward

Upper Body Push

Overhead Press (OHP)

Strict vertical pressing requiring supreme shoulder power and core rigidity.

Primary Muscles Recruited
Anterior/Medial Deltoid Upper Trapezius Triceps
Technical Execution Keys
  • Squeeze glutes and quads to create a rigid foundation
  • Keep vertical forearms directly beneath the bar
  • Bring head forward once bar clears the forehead
